# OpenTrestle

**Practical technology. Thoughtful engineering. Built for lasting ownership.**

OpenTrestle is an emerging technology initiative focused on AI systems, software, automation, and infrastructure. We are building toward technology that people and organizations can understand, operate, and control.

## Areas of focus

- **AI systems and automation** — useful, supervised workflows with clear human oversight.
- **Software and developer tools** — maintainable applications, integrations, and practical interfaces.
- **Private and hybrid infrastructure** — exploring local, customer-controlled, and hosted deployment options.
- **Operational engineering** — connecting technology to real work with reliability, security, and sensible documentation in mind.

## Related work

**SteadLamp** is a distinct Home, Life, and Business AI assistant and software product focused on durable context, permissions, and user control. Its architecture is designed to support replaceable AI providers rather than depend on a single infrastructure vendor.

**OpenTrestle Node** is an early-stage concept for packaged AI infrastructure and deployment options.

## How we work

We value clear boundaries, useful documentation, human accountability, privacy-conscious design, and systems that are practical to maintain.

This organization is being prepared for future public projects and documentation. Repository availability does not imply that a product or service is commercially launched.
